## Download and install

Download **PUSE-PSP-v0.3.0.zip** from the Assets section below.

1. Extract the complete `PUSE-PSP` folder.
2. Copy it to `ms0:/PSP/GAME/PUSE-PSP/` on the PSP Memory Stick.
3. Confirm that `EBOOT.PBP` and the `data` folder are both inside that folder.
4. Exit Pokemon Unbound and your GBA emulator completely.
5. Start **PUSE-PSP** from the PSP Game menu and choose the 128 KiB `.sav` or `.srm` file.

Use **O** to confirm and **X** to cancel. Keep a separate manual backup while
this release is experimental. PUSE-PSP also creates a `.puse.bak` file before
replacing a save.

## v0.3 highlights

- Live calculated stats when viewing a Party or PC Pokemon
- Live species, level, nature, IV, and EV previews inspired by PUSE Web
- Hidden Power type in the IV editor
- EV total and remaining display with always-on 31 IV, 252 per-stat EV, and 510 total EV limits
- Safe recovery for existing over-limit EV data: decreases are allowed while increases are blocked
- Triangle to set a legal maximum, Square to clear, and Select to compare against original stats
- Newly selected moves receive PP Max by default; Party moves are also refilled
- Calculated-stat preview on the final Pokemon creation screen
- Raw editing mode removed

Calculated Stats exclude held-item, ability, weather, status, and battle-stage
modifiers. The editor remains a structural tool, not an encounter, event,
trade-history, or competitive-legality certifier.
